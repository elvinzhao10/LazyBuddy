## Historical records

The remaining files in this directory preserve earlier plans, prompts, designs, evaluations, migration notes, templates, and reports. They are retained for provenance and study only; they are not current operating instructions and must not be treated as a dependency of a v0.15 installation.

- `plan/` and `prompts/` contain versioned execution records.
- `templates/`, `examples/`, and the named design/protocol/report files capture prior work.
- The repository `NOTICE` and `LICENSE` are the authoritative legal and attribution records.
